# Membership permission scope

- The new tenant-scoped capability catalog currently governs membership listing, eligible membership role/status management, and membership-access audit only. Other controllers still enforce their existing role and resource-scope rules; granting a membership capability does not grant access to unrelated ERP/LMS resources.
- Management has read-only membership access by default. Audit review and member changes require explicit grants; permission grants themselves remain limited to Admin and Independent Educator.
