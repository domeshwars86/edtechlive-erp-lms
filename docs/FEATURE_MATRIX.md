| People and roles | P0 | Maintain learners/staff; admins | Profile inputs → organization roster records | Student, Teacher, StudentGuardian, TeachingAssignment, Membership, OrganizationInvitation; roster, guardian management, invitation, `/my/student`, `/my/children` APIs | Admin/management/independent-educator role; student invitations require matching active roster; direct parent links require an active verified parent membership in the same tenant | Create/search/edit/update status, invitation expiry/revoke/accept, guardian link/unlink, primary contact uniqueness and tenant isolation | L | Partially implemented: branch-aware student/teacher rosters with create/edit/search/filter/status actions, teaching assignments, staff/student/parent invitations, and direct student/guardian link management; consent/retention workflows and roster reconciliation remain |

| Courses, lessons, materials | P0 | Deliver structured learning; teachers/students | Course details, module sequence, lesson text, private uploads → published learning content and student completion | Course, CourseModule, Lesson, Enrollment, CourseAsset, CourseLessonProgress; `/courses`, `/courses/{course}/modules`, `/courses/{course}/lessons`, `/progress`, `/assets`, `/enrollments` | Organization member; managers/linked teachers author and review scoped progress; enrolled students read published modules and lessons and change only their own completion; allow-listed file type/size; downloads re-authorized | Draft/publish lifecycle, linked teacher scope, enrollment isolation, module/lesson ordering, student visibility, completion percentage, private storage and download controls | L | Partially implemented: ordered module creation/editing, lesson grouping, course enrollment, private file upload/download, student completion, and course-scoped progress review; organization-wide reports, malware scanning/retention and durable long-term storage remain; assignments/assessments are tracked separately |
| Live and management meetings | P1 | Connect classes and staff | Time, organizer, group, secure provider link, consent-attested recording → scoped schedule and replay | Meeting, MeetingRecording; `/organizations/{id}/meetings` and `/meetings/{meeting}/recordings`; admin, teacher and family dashboards | Admin/management schedule; teachers require assignment and can manage recordings only for meetings they created; students and parents see only enrolled/linked groups and published recordings; HTTPS only; class overlaps rejected; uploads stay private | Role and tenant scope, assignment scope, overlap detection, file/link validation, consent attestation, publication and withdrawal, authenticated downloads | M/L | Partially implemented: manual HTTPS class/management meetings and staff-managed private recordings (MP4/MOV/WebM up to 50 MB or external HTTPS links), with publication limited to enrolled learners and explicitly linked families. No provider room creation, automatic capture, reminders, participant invites, retention purge or durable cloud storage configuration. |
| Assignments and exams | P1 | Practice and assess learning | Prompt, due date, text submission, score/feedback; scheduled objective and written-response quizzes; institution exam schedule, sectional paper, roster result and publication | CourseAssignment, AssignmentSubmission, CourseQuestionBank, CourseQuestionBankQuestion, CourseQuiz, QuizQuestion, QuizAttempt, AcademicExam, AcademicExamPaper, AcademicExamPaperSection, AcademicExamResult, AcademicExamSectionResult; question-bank/quiz/attempt APIs and `/exams` | Course managers/linked teachers author/reuse course questions and review written answers; enrolled students submit only to published courses and within quiz windows; school exam teachers need exact section/session/subject assignment; family results require publication and guardian links | Draft/publish, bank course scope, no duplicate/cross-course import, exact-set scoring, answer-key privacy, review-state privacy, opening/closing windows, attempt/time limits, score caps, full-roster coverage, section maximums equal paper maximum, section scores sum to total, immutable published marks | L | Implemented: assignment authoring, student text submission, teacher grading/feedback; editable searchable course-scoped question banks with paginated questions and snapshot import into draft quizzes; scheduled single-choice/multiple-select quizzes with difficulty, server scoring, proportional negative marking, optional pass threshold, time/attempt limits and paginated history; written-response quizzes with course-editor grading, feedback, partial/final scores, pending-review tracking and averages that exclude unreviewed attempts; institution exam schedules, subject papers with optional named/scored sections, per-section marks, teacher mark entry, absent/excused outcomes, roster-complete publication and family-visible score breakdowns. Exam series and additional analytics remain |
