# Roadmap

1. **Public website:** responsive brand and complete public routes, interactions, accessible behavior, static prerendering, production build and QA. **Frontend gate passed; legal/domain follow-ups remain.**
2. **Role-based dashboards:** Super Admin, Admin, Management, Teacher, Student, Parent and Independent Educator frontends. **Initial frontend gate passed; core admin, management, teacher, student, and parent flows are connected while other panels remain illustrative.**
3. **Backend foundation:** Laravel 13/Sanctum auth, email verification, password recovery, organization registration, tenant membership/role administration, and opt-in email notification queues/status are implemented. The latest 149-test/1,215-assertion API suite passes on SQLite; hosted PostgreSQL 16 CI and PostgreSQL verification for recent migrations remain.
4. **Connect APIs:** web login, account registration/recovery, session restore/logout and persistent active organization preference are connected; dashboard identity is real; academic, family, attendance, reporting, LMS and enquiry-review/reply workflows use scoped APIs. The allowlisted super-admin overview uses live aggregate counts and directory filters; organization suspension/reactivation is reasoned and audited through a dedicated paginated audit screen; broad platform analytics, account changes and other platform operations remain unfinished.
5. **Academic ERP:** branches assigned to classes, students and teachers with compatible teaching assignments, sessions, sections, searchable/filterable batches, subjects, fee categories, rosters, tenant role management and audit, staff/student/parent invitations, direct guardian link management, teacher-scoped attendance, student/guardian workspaces, homework, progress and results, weekly timetable, attendance reports, aggregate enrollment placement, teacher workload, manual expenses, net cash flow, bank reconciliation, and scoped study-document library have tenant-scoped APIs and web screens. Broader attendance correction workflows remain.
6. **LMS and assessment:** course/lesson authoring, enrollment, assignments, quizzes, submissions, results and progress. **Courses, ordered modules, lessons, enrollment, text assignments, student submissions, instructor grading, editable searchable course-scoped question banks with snapshot import, scheduled single-choice/multiple-select/written-response quizzes, exact-set scoring, configurable negative marking and pass thresholds, course-editor written-answer review, time/attempt limits, private study material, paginated result history, course-scoped learner progress, aggregate outcomes, institution exam schedules/papers with optional board-style sections and per-section marks, teacher grading, family result breakdowns, publication, paper/section summaries and CSV export are implemented; exam series and cross-exam analytics remain.**
7. **Free-first integrations:** manual class/management meeting scheduling with HTTPS links and private, consent-attested meeting recording upload/link, publication, withdrawal and scoped playback are implemented. Staff can prepare a prefilled WhatsApp chat from an announcement and review/send it manually. Workspace email delivery is opt-in, queued and tracked but stays disabled until an approved mail transport/worker is configured. Google Meet room provisioning/capture, reminders, participant invites, retention policy and push remain.
8. **Reports, payments, AI:** attendance, enrollment, teacher workload, course progress, published-quiz outcomes, assignment review, aggregate finance summaries, tenant-scoped CSV statement reconciliation, and CSV exports for academic outcomes, school exams, and finance summaries are implemented; add a sandbox payment gateway after provider selection and an optional AI provider adapter.
9. **Web QA and staging:** CI checks web typecheck/lint/build, a Playwright Chromium workflow smoke suite, Laravel API suite on SQLite and PostgreSQL, and mobile bundle. Browser E2E covers course discovery, mocked contact handling, local account verification-to-sign-in UI behavior, selected mobile viewports and a dashboard preview; live API browser verification, accessibility regression, PostgreSQL CI evidence, and authorized staging deployment remain.
10. **Mobile apps:** Expo client foundation and learner/teacher essentials now include secure login, courses, lessons, assignments, quizzes, grading, attendance, student/parent homework, lesson progress and assessment results, notifications, scoped meeting links and recent published meeting recordings, role-scoped published study materials, account-wide preferred organization selection, and Admin/Management student and teacher rosters, today's attendance, and aggregate course-progress overviews. Additional mobile reports and approvals, native device verification, and more admin/management workflows remain.
11. **Commercial readiness:** reviewed privacy/legal terms, operations, support, backups, monitoring and launch readiness.
12. **Future expansion:** entitlements, subscriptions and advanced features.

Academic ERP is in progress; its milestone is not complete until people, attendance, schedules, policies, and workflows have persistent APIs and validation. Do not claim the module complete before its gate. Calendar estimates are targets, not completion claims.
