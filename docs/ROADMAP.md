# Roadmap

1. **Public website:** responsive brand and complete public routes, interactions, accessible behavior, static prerendering, production build and QA. **Frontend gate passed; legal/domain follow-ups remain.**
2. **Role-based dashboards:** Super Admin, Admin, Management, Teacher, Student, Parent and Independent Educator frontends. **Initial frontend gate passed; core admin, management, teacher, student, and parent flows are connected while other panels remain illustrative.**
3. **Backend foundation:** Laravel 13/Sanctum auth, email verification, password recovery, organization registration and membership reads are implemented. The latest 121-test/917-assertion API suite and migrations pass on disposable PostgreSQL 17 and SQLite; hosted PostgreSQL 16 CI remains.
4. **Connect APIs:** web login, account registration/recovery, session restore/logout and persistent active organization preference are connected; dashboard identity is real; academic, family, attendance, reporting, LMS and enquiry-review/reply workflows use scoped APIs. The allowlisted super-admin overview uses live aggregate counts and directory filters; organization suspension/reactivation is reasoned and audited, while account changes and other platform operations remain unfinished.
5. **Academic ERP:** sessions, classes, sections, subjects, rosters, teacher assignments, staff/student/parent invitations, direct guardian link management, teacher-scoped attendance, student/guardian workspaces, homework, progress and results, weekly timetable, attendance reports, aggregate enrollment placement, teacher workload, manual expenses, net cash flow, and scoped study-document library have tenant-scoped APIs and web screens. Bank reconciliation and broader attendance correction workflows remain.
6. **LMS and assessment:** course/lesson authoring, enrollment, assignments, quizzes, submissions, results and progress. **Courses, ordered modules, lessons, enrollment, text assignments, student submissions, instructor grading, searchable course-scoped question banks with snapshot import, scheduled single-choice/multiple-select quizzes with difficulty tags, exact-set server scoring, time/attempt limits, private study material, paginated result history, course-scoped learner progress, aggregate outcomes, institution exam schedules/papers/teacher grading/family publication and session/subject summaries are implemented; bank question editing/deletion, subjective review and board-style exams remain.**
7. **Free-first integrations:** manual class/management meeting scheduling with HTTPS links is implemented. Google Meet room provisioning, recordings, email notifications, WhatsApp links and push remain.
8. **Reports, payments, AI:** attendance, enrollment, teacher workload, course progress, published-quiz outcomes, assignment review, aggregate finance summaries, tenant-scoped CSV statement reconciliation, and CSV exports for academic outcomes, school exams, and finance summaries are implemented; add a sandbox payment gateway after provider selection and an optional AI provider adapter.
9. **Web QA and staging:** CI checks web build/lint, Laravel API suite on SQLite and PostgreSQL, and mobile bundle. The PostgreSQL job awaits its first run; full authenticated browser/E2E, accessibility regression, and authorized staging deployment remain.
10. **Mobile apps:** Expo client foundation and learner/teacher essentials now include secure login, courses, lessons, assignments, quizzes, grading, attendance, student/parent homework, lesson progress and assessment results, notifications, scoped meeting links, role-scoped published study materials, account-wide preferred organization selection, and Admin/Management student and teacher rosters, today's attendance, and aggregate course-progress overviews. Additional mobile reports and approvals, native device verification, and more admin/management workflows remain.
11. **Commercial readiness:** reviewed privacy/legal terms, operations, support, backups, monitoring and launch readiness.
12. **Future expansion:** entitlements, subscriptions and advanced features.

Academic ERP is in progress; its milestone is not complete until people, attendance, schedules, policies, and workflows have persistent APIs and validation. Do not claim the module complete before its gate. Calendar estimates are targets, not completion claims.
