# Roadmap

1. **Public website:** responsive brand and complete public routes, interactions, accessible behavior, static prerendering, production build and QA. **Frontend gate passed; legal/domain follow-ups remain.**
2. **Role-based dashboards:** Super Admin, Admin, Management, Teacher, Student, Parent and Independent Educator frontends. **Initial frontend gate passed; core admin, management, teacher, student, and parent flows are connected while other panels remain illustrative.**
3. **Backend foundation:** Laravel 13/Sanctum auth, email verification, password recovery, organization registration and membership reads are implemented. The complete API suite now passes against a disposable PostgreSQL 17 database; hosted PostgreSQL 16 CI and policy-based permissions remain.
4. **Connect APIs:** web login, account registration/recovery, session restore/logout and persistent active organization preference are connected; dashboard identity is real; academic, family, attendance, reporting, LMS and enquiry-review records use scoped APIs. The allowlisted super-admin overview uses live aggregate counts with read-only organization and user directories; account changes and some platform operations remain unfinished.
5. **Academic ERP:** sessions, classes, sections, subjects, rosters, teacher assignments, staff/student/parent invitations, direct guardian link management, teacher-scoped attendance, student/guardian workspaces, homework, progress and results, weekly timetable, attendance reports, aggregate enrollment placement, teacher workload, manual expenses, net cash flow, and scoped study-document library have tenant-scoped APIs and web screens. Bank reconciliation and broader attendance correction workflows remain.
6. **LMS and assessment:** course/lesson authoring, enrollment, assignments, quizzes, submissions, results and progress. **Courses, ordered modules, lessons, enrollment, text assignments, student submissions, instructor grading, timed MCQ quizzes, auto-scored attempts, private study material, per-quiz result history, course-scoped learner progress, aggregate quiz/assignment outcomes, institution exam schedules/papers/teacher grading/family publication and session/subject exam summaries are implemented; board-style exam attempts remain.**
7. **Free-first integrations:** manual class/management meeting scheduling with HTTPS links is implemented. Google Meet room provisioning, recordings, email notifications, WhatsApp links and push remain.
8. **Reports, payments, AI:** attendance, enrollment, teacher workload, course progress, published-quiz outcomes, assignment review, aggregate finance summaries, and tenant-scoped CSV statement reconciliation with duplicate-statement protection are implemented; add a sandbox payment gateway after provider selection and an optional AI provider adapter.
9. **Web QA and staging:** CI checks web build/lint, Laravel API suite on SQLite and PostgreSQL, and mobile bundle. The PostgreSQL job awaits its first run; full authenticated browser/E2E, accessibility regression, and authorized staging deployment remain.
10. **Mobile apps:** Expo client foundation and learner/teacher essentials now include secure login, courses, lessons, assignments, quizzes, grading, attendance, student/parent homework, lesson progress and assessment results, notifications, scoped meeting links, role-scoped published study materials, and account-wide preferred organization selection. Native device verification plus more limited admin/management workflows remain.
11. **Commercial readiness:** reviewed privacy/legal terms, operations, support, backups, monitoring and launch readiness.
12. **Future expansion:** entitlements, subscriptions and advanced features.

Academic ERP is in progress; its milestone is not complete until people, attendance, schedules, policies, and workflows have persistent APIs and validation. Do not claim the module complete before its gate. Calendar estimates are targets, not completion claims.
