# Roadmap

1. **Public website:** responsive brand and complete public routes, interactions, accessible behavior, static prerendering, production build and QA. **Public catalog/detail pages now read eligible published courses from the API, and the detail-to-consented-enquiry flow carries editable course context; homepage examples remain illustrative and legal/domain follow-ups remain.**
2. **Role-based dashboards:** Super Admin, Admin, Management, Teacher, Student, Parent and Independent Educator frontends. **Initial frontend gate passed; core admin, management, teacher, student, and parent flows are connected while other panels remain illustrative.**
3. **Backend foundation:** Laravel 13/Sanctum auth, email verification, password recovery, organization registration, self-service name/email/password updates with current-password confirmation and fresh email verification after address changes, tenant-scoped membership capabilities and audit, reasoned platform account identity/status administration with API-wide suspended-account enforcement, audited recipient-controlled platform password-reset links, and opt-in email notification queues/status are implemented. Password changes revoke other API tokens. Membership capabilities currently cover membership workflows only; other resource policies remain role/scope based. The latest full API suite passed 209 tests / 1,807 assertions on in-memory SQLite; the previous 177-test suite passed on disposable PostgreSQL 17. Hosted PostgreSQL 16 CI remains.
4. **Connect APIs:** web login, account registration/recovery, session restore/logout, self-service account profile updates and persistent active organization preference are connected; dashboard identity is real; academic, family, attendance, reporting, LMS and enquiry-review/reply workflows use scoped APIs. The allowlisted super-admin overview uses live aggregate counts and directory filters; operators can update ordinary account identity with email re-verification, suspend/reactivate organizations and accounts, issue recipient-controlled password resets, and issue, review, reason-resend and reason-revoke staff invitations through recipient-confirmed account setup. Detailed role configuration and other platform operations remain unfinished.
5. **Academic ERP:** branches assigned to classes, students and teachers with compatible teaching assignments, create/list/edit/archive flows for sessions, classes, sections and subjects, searchable/filterable batches, fee categories, rosters, tenant role management and audit, staff/student/parent invitations, direct guardian link management, teacher-scoped attendance with reasoned correction history, student/guardian workspaces, homework, progress and results, weekly timetable, attendance reports, aggregate enrollment placement, teacher workload, manual expenses, net cash flow, bank reconciliation, and scoped study-document library have tenant-scoped APIs and web screens. Broader attendance workflows remain.
6. **LMS and assessment:** course/lesson authoring, enrollment, assignments, quizzes, submissions, results and progress. **Courses, ordered modules, lessons, enrollment, text assignments, student submissions, instructor grading, editable searchable course-scoped question banks with snapshot import, scheduled single-choice/multiple-select/written-response quizzes, exact-set scoring, configurable negative marking and pass thresholds, course-editor written-answer review, time/attempt limits, private study material, paginated result history, course-scoped learner progress, aggregate outcomes, institution exam schedules/papers with optional board-style sections and per-section marks, teacher grading, family result breakdowns, publication, paper/part summaries, class-section analytics, optional exam series with cross-session subject comparisons, percentage-point changes, and CSV export are implemented.**
7. **Free-first integrations:** manual class/management meeting scheduling, scoped private recordings, and optional Google OAuth room creation are implemented. Google OAuth client/API setup and live connection remain unverified. Staff can prepare a prefilled WhatsApp chat from an announcement and review/send it manually. Workspace email delivery is opt-in, queued and tracked but stays disabled until an approved mail transport/worker is configured. Meet capture, attendance sync, reminders, participant invites, retention policy and push remain.
8. **Reports, payments, AI:** attendance, enrollment, teacher workload, course progress, published-quiz outcomes, assignment review, aggregate finance summaries, tenant-scoped CSV statement reconciliation, and CSV exports for academic outcomes, school exams, and finance summaries are implemented. Razorpay Test Mode checkout now has server-verified, idempotent ledger settlement; live payments/refunds require a separately reviewed integration and operations plan. Optional local Ollama teacher lesson-plan and structured MCQ drafting is implemented with shared user quotas and metadata-only usage tracking; student tutoring, retrieval and additional provider adapters remain.
9. **Web QA and staging:** CI checks web typecheck, Vitest/React Testing Library component behavior, lint/build, Playwright Chromium public workflows and real account/session flow on disposable SQLite, Laravel API suite on SQLite and PostgreSQL, and mobile bundle. Browser E2E covers API-mocked public course discovery/detail and contextual contact submission, local account registration/email verification/session login/password reset, login-page verification resend, Admin membership permission grants, delegated Management member read-only access and notification inbox/read receipt, paginated formula-safe roster CSV export and reviewed field-level reconciliation, teacher-scoped exam section reporting/CSV export, Google Meet scheduling, attendance correction, platform account status/identity changes, invitation create/resend/revoke, mocked local-AI lesson-plan and MCQ drafting, Test Mode fee checkout, skip navigation, selected public mobile viewports and all seven dashboard previews; the latest full run passed 27 tests in 4.2 minutes. The starter 2-test unit suite verifies registration verification and login recovery. Broader component and accessibility regression, hosted PostgreSQL CI evidence, authenticated role workflows, and authorized staging deployment remain.
10. **Mobile apps:** Expo client foundation and learner/teacher essentials now include secure login, courses, lessons, assignments, quizzes, grading, assigned school-exam mark entry, attendance, student/parent homework, lesson progress and assessment results, notification inbox and account-wide notification preferences, role-scoped meeting creation, cancellation and links, recent published meeting recordings, role-scoped published study materials, account-wide preferred organization selection, self-service account name/email/password updates, searchable paginated active student and teacher rosters for Admin/Management/Independent Educator, organization-timezone attendance reports with editable date ranges, session/section enrollment and teacher-workload summaries for those roles, role-scoped course-progress, identity-free quiz/assignment outcomes, exam section-performance and series comparisons (teacher reports remain assignment-scoped by the API), plus read-only aggregate finance snapshots for Admin, Management and Independent Educators. Additional mobile approvals, native device verification, and more admin/management workflows remain.
11. **Commercial readiness:** reviewed privacy/legal terms, operations, support, backups, monitoring and launch readiness.
12. **Future expansion:** entitlements, subscriptions and advanced features.

Academic ERP is in progress; its milestone is not complete until people, attendance, schedules, policies, and workflows have persistent APIs and validation. Do not claim the module complete before its gate. Calendar estimates are targets, not completion claims.
