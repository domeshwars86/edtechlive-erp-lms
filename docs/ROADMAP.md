# Roadmap

1. **Public website:** responsive brand and complete public routes, interactions, accessible behavior, static prerendering, production build and QA. **Public catalog/detail pages read eligible published courses from the API, and verified students already on an organization's roster can self-enroll in its explicitly public courses; other visitors can send a contextual enquiry. Homepage examples remain illustrative and legal/domain follow-ups remain.**
2. **Role-based dashboards:** Super Admin, Admin, Management, Teacher, Student, Parent and Independent Educator frontends. **Initial frontend gate passed; core admin, management, teacher, student, and parent flows are connected while other panels remain illustrative.**
3. **Backend foundation:** Laravel 13/Sanctum auth, email verification, password recovery, organization registration, self-service name/email/password updates with current-password confirmation and fresh email verification after address changes, tenant-scoped membership and finance capabilities with audit, reasoned platform account identity/status administration, audited organization membership-role correction with API-wide suspended-account enforcement, audited recipient-controlled platform password-reset links, opt-in email notification queues/status, optional Expo push-device registration/queued delivery, and an optional S3-compatible private file disk are implemented. Password changes revoke other API tokens. Finance read/manage may be delegated to eligible staff memberships; other resource policies remain role/scope based. The latest full API suite passes **235 tests / 1,995 assertions** on in-memory SQLite; the prior full disposable PostgreSQL 17 run passed 230 tests / 1,962 assertions before student practice questions were added. Hosted PostgreSQL 16 CI remains unverified.
4. **Connect APIs:** web login, account registration/recovery, session restore/logout, self-service account profile updates and persistent active organization preference are connected; dashboard identity is real; academic, family, attendance, reporting, LMS and enquiry-review/reply workflows use scoped APIs; contact-enquiry retention supports dry-run review and an opt-in scheduled purge after policy approval. The allowlisted super-admin overview uses live aggregate counts and directory filters; operators can update ordinary account identity with email re-verification, suspend/reactivate organizations and accounts, issue recipient-controlled password resets, and issue, review, reason-resend and reason-revoke staff invitations through recipient-confirmed account setup. Detailed authorization policy configuration and other platform operations remain unfinished.
5. **Academic ERP:** branches assigned to classes, students and teachers with compatible teaching assignments, create/list/edit/archive flows for sessions, classes, sections and subjects, searchable/filterable batches, fee categories, rosters, tenant role management and audit, staff/student/parent invitations, direct guardian link management, teacher-scoped attendance with reasoned correction history, student/guardian workspaces, homework, progress and results, weekly timetable, attendance reports, aggregate enrollment placement, teacher workload, manual expenses, net cash flow, bank reconciliation, and scoped study-document library have tenant-scoped APIs and web screens. Broader attendance workflows remain.
6. **LMS and assessment:** course/lesson authoring, enrollment, assignments, quizzes, submissions, results and progress. **Courses, ordered modules, lessons, enrollment, text assignments, student submissions, instructor grading, editable searchable course-scoped question banks with snapshot import, scheduled single-choice/multiple-select/written-response quizzes, exact-set scoring, configurable negative marking and pass thresholds, course-editor written-answer review, time/attempt limits, private study material, paginated result history, course-scoped learner progress, aggregate outcomes, institution exam schedules/papers with optional board-style sections and per-section marks, teacher grading, family result breakdowns, publication, paper/part summaries, class-section analytics, optional exam series with cross-session subject comparisons, percentage-point changes, and CSV export are implemented.**
7. **Free-first integrations:** manual class/management meeting scheduling, scoped private recordings, optional Google OAuth room creation, and preference-aware reminders through the Laravel scheduler are implemented. Google OAuth client/API setup and live connection remain unverified. Staff can prepare a prefilled WhatsApp chat from an announcement and review/send it manually. Workspace email delivery is opt-in, queued and tracked but stays disabled until an approved mail transport/worker is configured. Expo push registration and queued delivery are implemented behind configuration; native build credentials/device delivery remain unverified. Meet capture, attendance sync, participant invites and recording retention policy remain.
8. **Reports, payments, AI:** attendance, enrollment, teacher workload, course progress, published-quiz outcomes, assignment review, aggregate finance summaries, tenant-scoped CSV statement reconciliation, and CSV exports for academic outcomes, school exams, and finance summaries are implemented. Razorpay Test Mode checkout now has server-verified, idempotent ledger settlement; live payments/refunds require a separately reviewed integration and operations plan. Optional local Ollama supports teacher lesson-plan/MCQ drafts, enrolled-student Q&A, and ungraded lesson-grounded practice questions, with shared user quotas and metadata-only usage tracking; uploaded-file retrieval and additional provider adapters remain.
9. **Web QA and staging:** CI checks web typecheck, Vitest/React Testing Library component behavior, lint/build, Playwright Chromium public workflows and real account/session flow on disposable SQLite, Laravel API suite on SQLite and PostgreSQL, and mobile bundle. Browser E2E covers API-mocked public course discovery/detail, verified-student public enrollment and contextual contact submission, local account registration/email verification/session login/password reset, login-page and public-course verification resend, Admin membership permission grants, delegated Management member read-only access and notification inbox/read receipt, paginated formula-safe roster CSV export and reviewed field-level reconciliation, teacher-scoped exam section reporting/CSV export, Google Meet scheduling, attendance correction, platform account status/identity and audited membership-role changes, invitation create/resend/revoke, mocked local-AI lesson-plan and MCQ drafting, student sign-in/course/study-help flow, including the AI learning-help shortcut, Test Mode fee checkout, skip navigation, selected public mobile viewports and all seven dashboard previews; the latest full serial run passed **35 tests in 3.5 minutes**. The 10-test component suite across five files covers account verification recovery, notices, learner study help/practice and report landing behavior. Broader authenticated workflows for additional roles and accessibility review, hosted PostgreSQL CI evidence, and authorized staging deployment remain.
10. **Mobile apps:** Expo client foundation and learner/teacher essentials now include secure login, courses, lessons, assignments, quizzes, grading, assigned school-exam mark entry, attendance, student/parent homework, lesson progress and assessment results, notification inbox and account-wide notification preferences, role-scoped meeting creation, cancellation and links, recent published meeting recordings, role-scoped published study materials, account-wide preferred organization selection, self-service account name/email/password updates, searchable paginated active student and teacher rosters for Admin/Management/Independent Educator, organization-timezone attendance reports with editable date ranges, session/section enrollment and teacher-workload summaries for those roles, role-scoped course-progress, identity-free quiz/assignment outcomes, exam section-performance and series comparisons (teacher reports remain assignment-scoped by the API), read-only aggregate finance snapshots, tenant-scoped family/staff invitation create/list/revoke, and permission-aware member search, role/status updates and access audit for authorized organization managers. Android/iOS JavaScript bundle exports and mobile typecheck pass locally; both exports are now included in CI, whose hosted run remains unverified. Native device builds/device verification, more mobile approvals and additional admin/management workflows remain.
11. **Commercial readiness:** reviewed privacy/legal terms, operations, support, backups, monitoring and launch readiness.
12. **Future expansion:** entitlements, subscriptions and advanced features.

Academic ERP is in progress; its milestone is not complete until people, attendance, schedules, policies, and workflows have persistent APIs and validation. Do not claim the module complete before its gate. Calendar estimates are targets, not completion claims.
